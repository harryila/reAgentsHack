## Implemented composition architecture

### Runtime composition receipt

`CorpusPipelineCompositionExternalReplayReceiptV1` rebuilds and binds:

- current extraction, verifier-core, and join-policy source bytes;
- installed runtime/dependency identity;
- grounding package and exact hosted bridge receipt;
- source manifest, corpus cutoff, publication and fragment membership;
- extraction execution context and terminal provider-call ledger;
- grounding replay, cohort reconciliation, typed corpus, and effective graph;
- a standard composed `PipelineFingerprintVerification` for calibration/release identity.

Every scientific, calibration, and release authority flag in this receipt remains false. The receipt
proves exact composition and lineage only.

### Receipt-bound corpus and policy identities

- `CompleteCorpusIdentityV2` binds v1 membership to the portable composition join.
- `CompleteCorpusIdentityV3` additionally embeds the full external-replay receipt and requires the
  current repository, package, bridge receipt, and loaded corpus to replay exactly.
- `ManifestCorpusPolicyBindingV3` binds the exact v3 corpus identity, claim-manifest hash, deployed
  policy hash, external receipt, composed calibration pipeline, and per-question join.

Extraction-only and composed release pipeline identities are intentionally different. Never compare
them as if they were hashes of the same surface.

### Public certificates

- Ordinary `literature-multiverse-verification-v8` is additive over v5. It replays the complete v5
  scientific/audit/calibration/stopping contract, then attaches the externally replayed composition,
  v3 corpus identity, and v3 manifest-policy binding. It may clear only
  `corpus_pipeline_identity_mismatch`.
- Outcome-free `literature-multiverse-condition-verification-v8` does the same over the exact v6
  common contract. It cannot contain held-out outcomes.
- Terminal `literature-multiverse-condition-verification-v9` binds an immutable v8 source, its
  receipt and v3 identities, the exact v6 witness, the recomputed v7 terminal witness, held-out
  assessment, and final decision. Terminal outcomes cannot rewrite the online state.
- Legacy v5/v6/v7 validators remain available for historical replay only.
